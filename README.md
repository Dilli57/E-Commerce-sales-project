📊 E-Commerce Sales Data Analysis
📝 Project Overview

This project focuses on analyzing E-Commerce sales data to uncover insights related to revenue, customer behavior, product performance, sales trends, and geographic distribution.
The goal is to help businesses make data-driven decisions to improve sales performance, optimize marketing strategies, and enhance customer experience.

🎯 Objectives

Perform data cleaning and preprocessing

Analyze sales performance across time, products, and regions

Identify top-selling products, best customer segments, and high-revenue regions

Detect seasonality, trends, and patterns in sales

Provide insights and recommendations for business growth

Build interactive dashboards using Excel / Power BI / Python

🔍 Key Insights

✨ December and January show peak sales (festival/holiday season)
✨ Furniture and Electronics generate maximum profit
✨ Customers from New York, California, and Texas drive highest revenue
✨ Repeat customers contribute 60% of total sales

💡 Business Recommendations

🚀 Offer discounts during low-sales months to boost performance
🛒 Focus marketing on top-performing regions and product categories
✨ Promote bundle offers for Electronics and Furniture
💬 Improve customer retention using loyalty programs
